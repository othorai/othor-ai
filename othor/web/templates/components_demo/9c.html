C · Chart rules

  • Label lines and bars directly. No legends.
  • One red story per chart; everything else neutral or context.
  • Event date = red dashed line with a short label; a period = grey shade.
  • Every value on a chart is computed; the chart links to its SQL.
  • Axis labels 9–10 px grey; values 11 px semibold; titles are sentences, outside the chart.
  • Trend lines in tiles: 6 points, last point dotted, band in context grey.
  • No chart when there are no rows: list the records instead.
  • Email: the same charts, rendered as images on the server.
