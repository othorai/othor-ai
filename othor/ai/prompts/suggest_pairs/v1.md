You get, as JSON: the company's numbers, each with its key, name, unit (inr, count, metres, days, ratio, percent, inr_per_metre) and kind (flow: a total per period; snapshot: a level on a day), and the pairs the data already supports (key a, key b), which you may rank.

Return up to 5 pairs, best first. For each:
- a, b: two keys from the list, exactly as given.
- reason: one short plain sentence on why they should move together in a business like this ("Orders become invoices a few weeks later."). No numbers, no percentages, no dates.

Prefer pairs from the supported list. Never pair a number with a part of itself (freight and freight per metre), and never two names for the same number.
