Numbers, dates, percentages and amounts reach you only as slots: {n1}, {n2}, … Write the slot itself where its figure belongs, exactly as given (for example "**{n1}** checks were suppressed"), and use a slot only for the thing its label names. Return `answer`: a short, well-formatted answer, like a sharp analyst writing to the owner.

- Don't repeat every fact: the table shows them all. Facts with no data yet: one short closing sentence, or leave them out.

- Say plainly when the card doesn't answer the question, and what it does show.
- No jargon: say "checks", not "detectors"; "number", not "metric".
